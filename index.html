Frequently asked questions

Is VGAMEING only for Minecraft?

No. The hub is designed for gaming across genres, including challenges, experiments, creator tools and video discoveries.

Do I need an account to use the site?

Most browsing and on-page tools are available without an account. Sign in when you want account-based features such as your player profile and saved-game library.

How do I change the site theme?

Use the sun/moon button near Sign In, or open the settings gear to choose Dark, Light, or System.

How do I install VGAMEING?

Open the settings gear and choose Install App on a compatible browser. If the browser does not offer installation, you can still bookmark the website.

Can I export my player data?

Open settings and choose Export. When signed in, the downloaded JSON includes profile details and the account data this page can read. It does not include private authentication credentials.

Are payments or paid subscriptions enabled?

No checkout or payment provider is connected by this feature pack. Do not enter payment details on this site.
